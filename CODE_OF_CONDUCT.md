# 📜 Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Project participants should create a respectful, constructive, and inclusive environment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be professional and courteous.
- Give technical feedback about the work, not the person.
- Explain disagreements with evidence.
- Respect privacy and attribution.
- Welcome questions from people with different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, and deliberate publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible.
