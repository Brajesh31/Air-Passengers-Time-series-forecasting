# 📜 Code of Conduct

## 1. Our commitment

The Air Passengers Time-Series Forecasting project aims to provide a constructive learning environment for people interested in Python, data analysis, statistics, and forecasting. Participants are expected to communicate respectfully and focus on improving the work.

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and public communication when someone is representing or participating in the project.

## 2. Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, professional, and considerate.
- Critique technical work, not the personal worth of contributors.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Welcome questions from people with different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual property.
- Give useful context when requesting changes.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct inaccurate claims.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Avoid overstating results or dismissing legitimate methodological concerns.

## 3. Unacceptable behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or targeted hostility.
- Personal attacks, insults, threats, or intimidation.
- Doxxing or publication of private information.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or review processes.
- Repeated abusive conduct after a request to stop.
- Publishing credentials, private datasets, or sensitive information.
- Knowingly misrepresenting another person's work or contributions.

## 4. Technical disagreement

Forecasting and statistical analysis involve assumptions and interpretation. Disagreement about model choice, metrics, data quality, or validation is expected. Keep the discussion evidence-based.

A contributor may challenge a result by identifying leakage, an invalid split, unsuitable metrics, a weak baseline, or unsupported claims. Such feedback is welcome when it is specific and respectful.

## 5. Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers should clarify expectations, review reports fairly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and take proportionate action when conduct violates this policy. Maintainers should avoid retaliation against people who raise concerns in good faith.

## 6. Reporting a concern

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Include relevant links, dates, and context. Do not post private evidence or sensitive personal information publicly.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as practical. The project does not promise a fixed response time, but serious safety or privacy concerns should be treated with appropriate care.

## 7. Possible responses

Depending on severity and context, responses may include:
- A request to stop the behaviour.
- A warning.
- Removal or editing of harmful content.
- Temporary restrictions on participation.
- Blocking or removal from project spaces.
- Escalation through the relevant platform reporting process.

The response should be proportionate and focused on maintaining a safe, constructive project environment.

## 8. Scope and interpretation

This policy is intended to support participation in the repository. It does not replace GitHub's terms, applicable law, or platform safety policies.
